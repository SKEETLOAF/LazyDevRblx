# Animation

Identify the rig and body mechanics, intended gameplay state, camera framing and action beats. Use reference motion to observe poses and timing, never to claim recovered original keyframes. Choose IK/FK/constraints, keyframes, curves and layers based on the action rather than a fixed recipe.

For each movement, evaluate silhouette, weight transfer, foot or prop contacts, anticipation where needed, follow-through, recovery, transitions and looping. Verify root placement, priorities, markers and how concurrent animations blend. Preserve animation tracks separately from VFX and audio cue ownership, synchronizing by meaningful events.

Eclipse or Moon may be available locally, but DevKit does not currently control their editor timelines. Use documented exported formats or manual handoff; never invent plugin methods.

**Quality evidence:** approved multi-view sequence, sample timing, structure/rig ownership, in-game playback and animation event results. Pixel-change peaks are not semantic action markers. Test at normal gameplay speed and in context.

**Blender facts:** The source audit can enumerate armatures, bone counts and assigned actions, but does not recover keyframe timing, demonstrate contact or prove playback. Judge motion from actual authorized sequences and runtime behavior.
